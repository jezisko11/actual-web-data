Weather forecast history

All values refer to about 15:00 Madrid time on the target date. Forecasts come from OpenWeatherMap's 3-hourly forecast (the slot nearest 15:00 local). "Actual" is the observation taken when the collector ran (about 15:00 local), not a daily average.

Water temperature

Satellite: Copernicus Marine Mediterranean high-resolution L4 SST (near real time), a night-time, gap-free analysis, i.e. an estimate from satellite infrared data (mean of sea cells within 2 km of the swimming point), not an in-water measurement. Buoy: Málaga coastal buoy (Puertos del Estado, station 1514); the daily value is the 06:00 UTC reading, and hourly readings appear about 33 minutes after the hour.

Credits: E.U. Copernicus Marine Service, CNR-ISMAR (satellite); Puertos del Estado, portus.puertos.es (buoy); OpenWeatherMap (weather).
